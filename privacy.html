Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026. Back to ACB Publisher

Who we are

ACB Publisher is a private tool operated by AGENT COMPANY Builder (ACB). It is used only by its operator, on the operator's own computer, for channels the operator owns.

Information the tool accesses

When the operator connects an account, the tool may access:

  • the account or channel identifier and name;
  • permission to upload videos, read the status of those uploads, and manage those videos and their captions;
  • permission to update the operator's own channel details, such as the description and banner;
  • statistics for the operator's own channels, such as views, watch time and estimated revenue;
  • comments on the operator's own videos, and permission to reply to or moderate them, where the platform permits it.

For YouTube, the tool requests these Google permissions: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, youtube, youtube.force-ssl, yt-analytics.readonly and yt-analytics-monetary.readonly.

How the information is used

The information is used only to publish and manage the operator's videos and channels, confirm videos were published, and produce performance reports for the operator. Statistics may be included in the operator's own AI-assisted reports.

The tool does not sell this information, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

Storage and security

Access tokens are encrypted with Windows Data Protection and stored on the operator's computer. Other data stays on that computer. The tool has no public server and no user database.

Sharing

Information is sent only to the platform it belongs to, in order to perform the action the operator requested. It is not shared with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

YouTube API Services

ACB Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

Access to a Google account can be removed at any time at Google security settings. Similar controls exist in Facebook and TikTok account settings.

To ask for your data to be deleted, email lienheben@gmail.com. The request will be handled within 30 days.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
