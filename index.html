ACB Publisher

A private publishing tool operated by AGENT COMPANY Builder (ACB).

What it does

ACB Publisher runs on the operator's own computer. It lets the operator upload and schedule videos for channels the operator owns, and read those channels' statistics so the operator can see how each video performs.

Every public post is reviewed and approved by the operator before it is published. The tool is not offered to the public and does not manage accounts belonging to anyone else.
